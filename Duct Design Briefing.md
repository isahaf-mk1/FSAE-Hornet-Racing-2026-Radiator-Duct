# Duct Design
## FSAE Cooling Design Scoring
- Heat transfer vs. velocity, mass airflow, heat transfer coefficients <br/>
- Size and placement of radiators, fans, ducting, duct area ratios, velocity vs. pressure <br/>
- Impact on aerodynamics, drag, and downforce; cooling vs. aero tradeoff <br/>

## Design Focus
- Optimizing the geometry of the duct for inlet size vs. mass flow rate
- Maintain high pressure throughout the outlet of the duct

## Design Decisions
- Fin density of the radiator is 30 fins/inch, classifying it as a high fin density radiator. The large surface area increases flow resistance, requiring a higher pressure differential to maintain optimal flow rate. <br/>
- A longer duct with shallower angle was selected based on “Performance Prediction of Straight Two-Dimensional Diffusers” (Greywall, M. S. 1980). <br/>
- Determined  67% inlet area to outlet area based on high delta pressure theory from “Automobile Aerodynamics” (Muto, S. 1985). <br/>
- Duct geometry was designed to maintain low expansion angles and avoid flow separation. <br/>
- Due to packaging constrains, especially the location of electrical panel, a low pressure zone develops on one side of the radiator duct. <br/>
- Duct geometry optimized by performing a physical sweep of inlet area and outlet area to determine the lowest internal drag coefficient. <br/>
