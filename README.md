# Hornet Racing 2026 Radiator Duct and Cooling Optimization

## Summary
Designed a radiator sidepod to improve mass airflow through the vehicle cooling system and resolve thermal performance deficiencies. Performed CFD analysis and validated system performance through tuft testing and back-to-back vehicle endurance testing.

## Design Considerations
- Along the driver side of the car <br/>
- Must cover the Haltech ECU <br/>
- Must be at least 75mm from the rear and front wheel <br/>
- Can only extend up to the outside edge of the wheels <br/>
- Edges of the electrical panel cover section of the duct must be tangent to top and bottom frame pipes

## Results
Average coolant temperature reduction of 11%
